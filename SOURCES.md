## Picture cards

**COP31 runs into overtime in fossil-fuel row** — Associated Press,
Health Policy Watch. The Antalya talks ran into a second day of
overtime on Saturday; the last-minute draft presented by COP31
President Murat Kurum contains no mention of fossil fuels or a
phase-out; the EU, the Marshall Islands and Panama objected; at over
44 hours it is the longest overrun in the summit's history.

**Drones strike Russian cargo ship; crew rescued** — CNN, Reuters.
Two drones struck the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of
Finland on Saturday morning, St Petersburg → Kaliningrad; all 16 crew
evacuated; the US-sanctioned, Oboronlogistika-owned vessel anchored
off Loviisa with Finland leading the rescue; Finland's military said
Ukraine was likely behind the attack.

**Tanker attacks reach a wartime high** — AFP, Financial Times.
UKMTO reported two more tankers hit near Khasab, Oman, on Saturday.
UKMTO's weekly figures: 263 vessels transited the Strait of Hormuz
Nov 12–18, 15% fewer than the prior week; only 37 were oil tankers,
down from 52.

**Turnout falls on sixth weekend of protests** — Reuters, AFP.
Largely peaceful cost-of-living and student marches across France;
Interior Ministry: 82,500 nationwide including 21,500 in Paris;
organisers claimed 150,000; about 70,000 security forces deployed;
sporadic violence in Marseille.

## Briefs

**Missile strike kills two in Zelensky's hometown** — Associated
Press, New York Post. A Russian ballistic missile strike on Kryvyi
Rih on Saturday killed 2 and wounded 60.

**States agree to draft 'killer robot' rules** — Reuters, AFP. The
CCW talks in Geneva ended early on Saturday; the 128 states parties
agreed to launch a two-year elaboration of an international instrument
on lethal autonomous weapons; about 100 countries back legally binding
rules; the US, Russia and Israel are opposed.

**FBI most-wanted cartel boss captured** — Reuters, CNN. Fausto
Isidro Meza Flores ("El Chapo Isidro"), on the FBI's ten most-wanted
list with a $5m reward, was captured in Guasave, Sinaloa, in an
operation with US intelligence support; Mexico's defence ministry
announced it on Saturday; clashes killed at least 11.

**Strong earthquake rattles the capital** — Associated Press, AFP.
Magnitude 6.7 (USGS; Peru's Geophysical Institute) at 00:09 GMT
Saturday, about 34 km north of Junín, deep; felt strongly in Lima;
minor damage, no early fatality reports.

**Child dies of measles in Virginia** — Associated Press, Reuters. A
child under five is Virginia's first measles-related death of 2026.
(No state case count is printed: outlets reported conflicting totals.)

**Cyclone Akvan declared over** — PTI, India Meteorological
Department. The IMD said on Saturday the storm was over, its remnant
low over north interior Odisha; about 440,000 people had been
evacuated; no lives lost, no major damage.

**Trump: any Iran deal must be 'permanent'** — CBS News, Fox News.
President Trump told Fox News on 21 November he wants a deal that is
"going to be permanent"; Tehran says messages still pass through
mediators in Qatar and Pakistan.

**EU and Indonesia sign trade deal** — Reuters, ANTARA. The EU and
Indonesia signed the Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement and
an Investment Protection Agreement in Brussels on Saturday after
10 years of negotiations; duties removed on more than 90% of
products; expected to take effect in early 2027.

**Search goes on after ferry disaster** — Reuters, NEMA. The crowded
barge "Love Boat", carrying funeral-goers, capsized on the Nun River,
Bayelsa State, on Friday; Saturday's story is the continuing search:
NEMA's provisional toll, released Saturday, was 24 dead and 24
rescued. (Higher local figures were reported but not adopted.)
